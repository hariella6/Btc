BALeb is an experimental decision-support prototype, not financial advice. No orders are placed. Exchange prices can diverge from Kalshi's settlement benchmark (many crypto contracts use CF Benchmarks and specific averaging rules). Never treat cross-exchange spot price as the official settlement value. Coinbase and Kraken are independent exchange references, not CF Benchmarks settlement data. TradingView may be used as a separate visual cross-check but is not an API feed into this model. Live connections depend on browser/network CORS and WebSocket availability. No forecast has been backtested or calibrated yet.
